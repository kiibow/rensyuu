# うえの弁護士事務所 — 「ほどける線」 Treatment

Style: One-line Drawing (styles/one-line). 1920×1080, 24 fps, ~30 s. No voice (written subtitles in Japanese only). Use: website hero / waiting-room screen / social.
Content rule: no factual claims about the firm (fees, specialties, results, contact) — only an invitation to consult. Contact line left for the firm to add.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Knot → line (chosen).** A tangled scribble (a worry) slowly unravels into one calm line that leads to a consultation table and on to a road ahead. Turn = the knot loosens. Fits "one line" natively: transformation + stopping is an event.
2. Two lines from opposite corners (client and lawyer) drawn in alternation until they meet in a handshake. Nice, but "meet" reads as a sales handshake; colder.
3. Line as graph: an anxiety "heartbeat" line that calms down after consultation. Clear, but medical; less about law.
Chose 1: the medium itself (one line that can't be cut, only untangled) is the message — "もつれた問題も、一本の線にほどける".

## Logline & arc
A pen racing across paper ties itself into a knot; it stops, ink pools; then, slowly, the line finds its way out, draws two people across a table, and runs on as a clear road toward a door.
Setup (knot) → turn (stop, silence, untangle) → ending (open door + firm name, written).

## Benchmark
Learn: the confident single gesture of light-drawing photography; ad films for public-service consultation lines (calm pace, one idea). Not taken: any character, shot or melody.

| 6 | 24–31 | continuous pull-back reveals whole picture; title written on empty paper | everything was one line |

## Cue map
Solo plucked/bowed-like string (Karplus–Strong, warm nylon tone), 72 bpm (beat 0.833 s), D major, one melodic line.
- 0–4.5: short fast notes following corners of the scribble, rising, slightly unstable (chromatic neighbour notes).
- 4.5–6.2: two falling notes; 6.2: everything stops — literal silence ~2 s (pen and music off).
- 8.2: first sound after silence = a single low D, long — the line starts to untangle.
- 9–19.5: slow singing line, notes on marks (each figure's head, the table).
- 19.5–24: a little faster, walking pace.
- 24–31: highest note (A5) when the door accent is completed / title, resolving to D; ring out.
