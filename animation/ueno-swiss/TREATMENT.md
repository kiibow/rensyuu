# うえの弁護士事務所 — 「整う」 Treatment (Swiss Motion Graphics)

Revision of the one-line trial: the client wants a proper corporate look. 1920×1080, 24 fps, 36 s, 100 bpm (bar 2.4 s). No voice; Japanese type is the subtitle layer. One family: Noto Sans JP (OFL), weights 400/500/700/900.
Palette: page #F1F0EC, paper #FFFFFF, ink #111111, greys #D6D4CE/#E4E2DC, signal ultramarine #1F3FFF (trust, calm). No claims about fees, specialties or results; the three steps are a generic consultation flow.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The exception finds its place (chosen).** A single blue square (the client's problem) drifts off-grid, linear and unsnapped, while the firm's system (grid, statement, 3 steps) builds around it on the beat; after a silence it snaps onto the grid and becomes the mark beside the firm's name.
2. A 24-row timetable of a day at the firm — needs facts we don't have.
3. Split-screen comparison "alone vs. with a lawyer" — reads as advertising pressure.

## Logline / arc
Something doesn't fit (square off-grid) → a calm system lays out the steps → silence → it snaps into place → the firm's name.

## Score / sound
Kalimba-like modal ostinato on 8ths (D minor pentatonic → resolves to F major at the snap), synthesized kick on beats from bar 1, hats on offbeats from bar 4, pizz bass from bar 4. Hard stop at 25.5; the snap at 26.4 = thunk + full band back on F. Foley: paper slap on every snap, numeral thunk on each digit step, ruling-pen tss on grid lines. −14 LUFS, grain 0.
